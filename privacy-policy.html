2. Information We Collect

A. Account and Profile Information

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, username, player/game ID, profile information and tournament-related information.

B. Tournament Information

We may collect information required to register and manage tournaments, including team/player details, slot information, match information, scores, rankings, results and prize-related records.

C. Payment and Transaction Information

If you make a payment or receive a prize, transaction-related information may be processed. We do not intend to store complete card, UPI PIN, CVV or banking passwords on our servers. Payment details may be handled by the relevant payment service provider.

D. Device and Technical Information

We and our service providers may receive technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, crash information and general usage information when necessary to operate, secure and improve the service.

E. Communications

If you contact support or communicate through features provided by the app, we may receive the information you submit, such as your email address, message and attachments.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts and profiles.
  • Register players and teams for esports tournaments.
  • Provide match schedules, room information, scores, leaderboards and tournament results.
  • Process or facilitate payments, refunds and prize-related administration where applicable.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Send important service, tournament and account notifications.
  • Show and measure advertisements where advertising is enabled.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, cheating, security incidents and violations of our rules.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve our services.
  • Comply with applicable laws and lawful requests.

4. Advertising

Our app may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These providers may collect or use certain device, advertising and usage information to provide, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

Where required, we will provide applicable choices or consent controls for personalized advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for functions such as authentication, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, payments, notifications and advertising. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support or improve the service, or when required by law.

Some information may be visible to other users or the public when you participate in a tournament. This can include player/team names, game IDs, match participation, scores, rankings, results and other tournament-related information. Do not submit information that you do not want displayed publicly.
